Reference: How timing is calculated

Calculation flow

Engine

Timing source

Location sunrise/sunset, manual sunrise/sunset, or traditional fallback 06:00 / 18:00.

Day and Night

Day = Sunrise → Sunset
Night = Sunset → Next Sunrise

Samam

Day ÷ 5
Night ÷ 5

Activity

Activity duration = Samam duration × units ÷ 144

Inner parts

Inner duration = Activity duration ÷ 5

Moon blocks

Rising: Block 1–15 from 0°→180°. Waning: Block 1–15 from 180°→360°.

Unit tables

Total 144

Rising Day: Eat 30, Walk 36, Rule 48, Sleep 18, Death 12.

Rising Night: Eat 30, Rule 48, Death 12, Walk 36, Sleep 18.

Waning Day: Eat 48, Death 30, Sleep 12, Rule 18, Walk 36.

Waning Night: Eat 42, Sleep 18, Walk 42, Death 24, Rule 18.
